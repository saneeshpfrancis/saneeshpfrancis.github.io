Level II — Understand

Level III — Apply

Measure an unknown prism's refractive index

The real spectrometer procedure: rotate the prism table until the sodium line stops reversing direction and hovers at its calmest point — that's the angle of minimum deviation, Dm. Then apply the formula.

Procedure

Set the apex angle

This bench uses a standard 60° prism (A = 60°), already measured for you.

Hunt for minimum deviation

Drag the prism-table slider below. Watch the live deviation readout — rotate until it stops falling and just begins to rise again. That turning point is Dm.

Record Dm

Click "Lock this as my reading" at the lowest value you can find.

Calculate μ

Use μ = sin[(A + Dm)/2] / sin(A/2) and compare with the bench's hidden answer.
